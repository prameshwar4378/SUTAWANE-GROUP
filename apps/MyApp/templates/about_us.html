✦ THE SUTAWANE GENESIS ✦

From a Humble Room to Vaijapur's Premier Benchmark

In 2011, Prof. S. R. Sutawane recognized a persistent dilemma faced by talented students in regional towns: while students possessed immense hunger and intellect, they lacked access to high-caliber, structured entrance coaching without migrating to distant, expensive metropolitan hubs like Kota or Pune.

With a small cohort of 25 passionate students and a relentless commitment to concept clarity, Sutawane Classes was born. Today, that small gathering has blossomed into an institution that has empowered over 10,000 alumni, consistently producing taluka toppers, state rankers, doctors, engineers, and chartered accountants.

✦ 15 YEARS OF TRIUMPH ✦

Milestones in Our Journey

A fifteen-year chronicle of relentless pedagogical innovation, student triumphs, and institutional expansion.

2011 🌱

Inception & Concept-First Philosophy

Founded in Vaijapur with 25 secondary school students. Introduction of our proprietary chapter-wise subjective test series that quickly established our academic reputation.

2015 🏆

First Taluka Rank 1 in State Board

Our student scored 98.6% in 10th SSC State Board, securing the overall Taluka Rank 1. First dedicated entrance preparatory wing launched for 11th Science students.

2018 🏛️

Main Campus Facility Inauguration

Inaugurated our multi-story Ladgaon Road facility featuring air-conditioned smart classrooms, science practical observation desks, and a dedicated reference library.

2021 ⚡

State Percentile Landmark (99.82%ile in MHT-CET)

Record-breaking entrance results with peak percentile of 99.82%ile. Over 40 students admitted into premier government engineering (COEP, VJTI) and medical colleges across Maharashtra.

2026 🚀

Smart Cloud Coaching & AI-Enabled Practice

Integration of digital attendance tracking, live WhatsApp test reporting, high-yield digital study modules, and state-of-the-art interactive flat panels across all classrooms.

The 4 Academic Pillars of Sutawane Classes

Every student's journey is governed by our battle-tested educational framework that guarantees conceptual depth and exam performance.

PILLAR 01

Concept First Principles

We teach the fundamental "why" behind every formula before introducing speed shortcuts, ensuring students can solve even unfamiliar twists in exam questions.

PILLAR 02

Daily Practice Sheets (DPPs)

Every single lecture is paired with a mandatory Daily Practice Problem sheet submitted the next morning for rigorous habit formation.

PILLAR 03

OMR Proctored Mock Tests

Simulated weekly exam drills with strict negative marking, timer discipline, and computerized optical mark evaluation for exam confidence.

PILLAR 04

1-on-1 Faculty Doubt Desk

Daily dedicated post-lecture doubt counters where students sit face-to-face with subject masters until weak concepts are completely resolved.
